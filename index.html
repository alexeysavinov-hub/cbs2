About the game

Crown Dawn is a competitive strategy game on a hex map. Capture territory, deploy heroes from your deck and take the crown from real players or AI before the timer runs out. Short rounds, real decisions.

A small team. A long game.

Cybersystems is a small team that loves strategy games. We believe a good mobile game should be easy to start, interesting to learn and enjoyable to return to.

We are based in Nicosia, Cyprus, and take care of our games at every stage — from the first idea to updates after release.

Crown Dawn is our first title, and we continue to develop it. Merge Arena is next.
