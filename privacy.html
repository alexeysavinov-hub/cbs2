Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Cybersystems VAD LTD (“we”, “us”) processes when you visit this website or play our mobile games, and what rights you have. Last updated: 25 September 2026.

1. Who we are

Cybersystems VAD LTD, Kallipoleos 3, 1st floor, Office 102, 1055 Nicosia, Cyprus. Email: admin@cybersystems.pro. We are the data controller for the processing described here.

2. This website

The website is a static site with no user accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We do not set cookies and do not use third-party tracking.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in its server logs for security and operational purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When they load, your browser sends a request (including your IP address) to Google. See Google’s privacy policy.

3. When you contact us

If you email or call us, we process the contact details and the content of your message in order to reply and handle your request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or performance of a contract where the enquiry concerns one. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up.

4. Our games

Our games (currently Crown Dawn; Merge Arena is in development) are distributed through Google Play. Depending on the game and version, the following data may be processed:

  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system version, language, app version, IP-derived approximate region.
  • Identifiers — an installation ID generated by the game, and the Google Advertising ID where advertising is used confirm.
  • Gameplay data — progress, match results, in-game purchases and settings, used to run the game, save progress and balance matchmaking.
  • Crash and performance data — diagnostic reports that help us fix bugs.
  • Purchases — in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive order confirmations but never your payment card details.

We use the following third-party services inside our games confirm the list against the current build: Google Play Services and Google Play Billing; analytics and crash reporting providers; and, where the game shows ads, advertising networks. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

Legal bases: performance of the contract with you (running the game, saving progress, purchases), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, fixing bugs, understanding how the game is used) and your consent where required, for example for personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time in the game settings or in your device’s privacy settings.

5. Children

Our games and website are not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers named above, with Google Play as the distribution platform, and where required by law. Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area; in that case transfers rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

7. Retention

Gameplay and account data are kept while you play and for a reasonable period afterwards so that you can return to your progress. Diagnostic data is kept for a limited period needed to analyse it. Data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed, or earlier at your request.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. To exercise these rights, email admin@cybersystems.pro. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus or your local supervisory authority.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when our games or services change. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
